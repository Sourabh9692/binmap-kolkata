# Field protocol v0.1

1. Confirm the public map pin and safe entrance connection. Review OSM paths before relying on them.
2. Choose a manageable set of segments. The full 500 m network may contain much more than a single kilometre of walking.
3. Use a pseudonym. Collect only necessary data. Avoid faces, homes, number plates and identifying details in photographs.
4. Walk both sides where safely possible. Record obstructions, partial coverage and inability to inspect honestly. Do not enter private areas to improve coverage.
5. Record positive bins separately, including category, condition, public accessibility, GPS uncertainty and date. Do not count household/community containers as pedestrian bins by default.
6. Record “no bin observed” only after inspection. The review rule requires both sides, clear visibility and ≥95% declared coverage. v0.1 does not independently verify traversal; arrange a second inspection of a sample before municipal reporting.
7. Save offline drafts; do not clear browser data. Sync using the survey key. A failed upload leaves the draft available.
8. A reviewer checks GPS/photo/segment consistency and the observation time. Reject poor accuracy, missing context, contradictory evidence or unjustified negative conclusions. Rejection is preserved in the audit log; corrected evidence gets a new ID.
9. Revisit stale records (default: 30 days) and changed conditions. Shared review keys do not establish independent reviewer identity; record independent checks in the project research log.
10. Export only reviewed, privacy-safe results. Include the method, snapshot date, uncertainty, source attribution and exact surveyed extent.

No actual field inspection has been performed by the implementation process. All automated-test evidence lives in temporary test databases.
